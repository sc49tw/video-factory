N001-S1 — N001

Arc: one held threshold situation in which the body's limits, the disappearing destination, and the thing that is still there are all asked at once

Function: Carry all three opening questions inside a single continuous situation so that what is still there lands through duration, framing, and attention rather than through a second symbolic image.
Intent: One uninterrupted situation held across the full block: a person at seated height on the edge of a doorway, a corridor, or a path, with a hand on a chair arm or a foot at a step, and open ordinary space ahead that they are not taking. The camera stays close enough to the body to register the limit and wide enough to keep the possibility of movement in frame, and the stillness deepens as the questions accumulate rather than cutting away to illustrate them. Level, observational, no face required.

Avoid: wheelchair portraits and clinical stock; wrinkle and age-spot close-ups; skeleton, hourglass, or clock metaphors; grey-haired slow-motion hero shots; one literal image per rhetorical question; a second or third symbolic shot to illustrate each question; existential-film emptiness: cleared rooms, a lone lit lamp, an empty chair, or a figure dwarfed by negative space; memory or flashback montage and sepia aging effects
