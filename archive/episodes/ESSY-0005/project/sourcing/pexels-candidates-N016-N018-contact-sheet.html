N016-S1 — N016

Arc: regret held without content -> the future self making a claim -> two incompatible rhythms of a life left equally standing

Function: Name regret as a weight the narrator carries, while keeping its content deliberately unspecified so no particular past choice is identified as the wrong one.
Intent: The narrator alone with a long, unresolved thought: a person sitting somewhere familiar and ordinary, turning something over in their mind without any object in frame identifying what it is about. The hesitation is real and the mood is heavy, but the material of the regret is withheld from the image exactly as it is withheld from the narration. Whatever the regret is about, the audience is not told.

Avoid: travelling, tourism, or a journey never taken; unused tickets, passports, suitcases, or unvisited destinations; any specific life choice depicted as the mistaken one; crying and self-flagellation; storm-cloud and rain-gutter imagery; sepia flashback treatment; pictorial regret metaphors

N018-S2 — N018

Arc: an ordinary fragment of a younger life, seen without verdict -> time simply continuing -> the remaining road travelled rather than surveyed

Function: Withhold explanation and let the narration carry the self-forgiveness, using stillness and continuation rather than any symbolic act of release.
Intent: The same young person simply continuing: a stretch of ordinary time passing with nothing resolved, nothing dropped, and nothing surrendered — a held frame as the day carries on and the person is still in it. No gesture completes anything.
