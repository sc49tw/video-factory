N011-S1 — N011

Arc: an actual eighty-year-old person inside an ordinary weekday morning -> the reason to get out of bed not yet answering itself

Function: Establish the imagined future as a real person living a real Tuesday: the emphasis is on an actual eighty-year-old in their own home, not on an empty room standing in for old age.
Intent: A genuinely elderly person of around eighty at home on an ordinary weekday morning, already up and going through it: kettle on, curtains, weather, a mug carried, ordinary clothes, a home arranged by someone who actually lives in it.
